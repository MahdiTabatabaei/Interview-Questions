### AddSingleton

ثبت یک سرویس در DI container.
```csharp

builder.Services.AddSingleton<IMyService, MyService>();

```
---

### AddHostedService

ثبت **background service**
```csharp

builder.Services.AddHostedService<Worker>();

```
